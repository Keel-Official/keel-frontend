# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Ambassador and SCF reviewers, Stellar ecosystem builders, and prospective technical users. They arrive with varying levels of Web3 familiarity and need to understand why a quoted price can exist without enough executable liquidity behind it, then inspect the evidence, methodology, and case study.

## Product Purpose

Keel is a read-only liquidity-risk intelligence layer for Stellar. It measures executable market depth, estimates manipulation cost and reachability, and surfaces collateral risk in terms that can be checked and reproduced. The landing page succeeds when a first-time visitor can explain Keel in under 30 seconds and can find the assets, methodology, API contract, and Blend/USTRY case study.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, accountable. Keel should feel like a research instrument: technically credible without being hostile to non-technical readers. Copy should distinguish observed evidence from inference and keep uncertainty visible.

## Anti-references

Avoid speculative crypto aesthetics, casino or meme-coin energy, neon cyberpunk treatment, hype language, wallet/transaction patterns, unexplained scores, production-SLA claims, and false certainty. Do not imply Keel guarantees exploit prevention or is sufficient on its own for a financial decision.

## Design Principles

- Explain the gap between quoted price and executable liquidity within seconds.
- Show evidence before abstraction: conclusion, reason, evidence, then methodology and provenance.
- Preserve uncertainty and source quality as first-class information.
- Use precise units and language instead of decorative financial theater.
- Make the product easy to inspect: public methodology, reproducible evidence, and permanent read-only behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Risk states must be communicated with text and structure, never color alone. Maintain visible keyboard focus, logical heading hierarchy, readable contrast, descriptive labels, touch targets of at least 44px where practical, and reduced-motion alternatives. Keep the explanatory copy accessible to visitors who do not know Stellar, SDEX, AMMs, or oracle terminology.
